## /overview

Sparki is a veterinary practice-management platform designed from the **workflow outward**.

Instead of starting with screens and forcing clinic teams into them, Sparki begins with the actual movement of patients, staff, medical information, inventory, and communication throughout the day.

The platform is being built around real veterinary workflows with direct clinical input, with the goal of making day-to-day operations feel faster, clearer, and less fragmented.

> **This repository is a public product and engineering preview.**  
> The production Sparki codebase remains private.
